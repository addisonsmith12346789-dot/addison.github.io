About Haunted Virginia

Haunted Virginia is a website created to explore the history, legends, and reported hauntings found throughout Virginia.

Virginia has a long and fascinating history, and many locations throughout the state have stories involving ghosts, unexplained events, and unusual experiences. This website brings some of those locations together in one place so visitors can learn more about the stories connected to them.

The goal of Haunted Virginia is to make learning about Virginia's haunted history interesting and easy to explore. Visitors can discover different locations, learn about their backgrounds, and explore the stories that have made these places memorable.
